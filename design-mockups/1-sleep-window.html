      Is this for you?

      It is if you have had trouble falling asleep, staying asleep, or waking too early for several weeks or longer.

      CBT-I (cognitive behavioral therapy for insomnia) is what sleep medicine guidelines recommend first for chronic insomnia, before sleeping pills. This course teaches it in plain steps you can do at home.

      See a doctor instead, or as well, if

      • your symptoms are severe
      • you get drowsy while driving
      • you think you might have sleep apnea or restless legs

        “Going to bed later sounds backwards. It’s the most effective tool we have.”

        Wendy. When she had insomnia, she started with a 1:00 AM bedtime. Now she goes to bed at 11 to 11:30 PM.

        The idea

        The longer you are awake, the more sleep pressure builds. Compression moves your bedtime later, so that when you lie down you have enough pressure to sleep straight through.

        Your wake-up time stays fixed, every day. Within days to a few weeks your sleep consolidates into one solid block. Then you move bedtime earlier in small steps.

        Pair it with stimulus control

        If you are in bed during your window and can’t sleep, get up and leave the room after 20 minutes. Come back when you feel sleepy. Module 5 explains why.

        Bob’s schedule, night by night

        Bob was in bed from 9:00 PM to 6:30 AM, 9.5 hours, but asleep for only 5. He kept 6:30 AM as his wake time and moved bedtime to 1:00 AM: his 5 hours of sleep plus 30 minutes. The window narrows first, then widens as his sleep fills it.

        Set your own schedule in 5 steps

        1. Pick your wake-up time. One you can keep every day, weekends included.
        2. Work out your average actual sleep. Use a week of your sleep diary. Count 5 hours if it comes out lower.
        3. Set your bedtime: wake time minus your sleep, minus 30 minutes.
        4. Hold that schedule until you sleep through it most nights.
        5. Move bedtime 30 minutes earlier and repeat, until you sleep 6 to 7.5 hours. If your sleep gets shaky, step back for a few days.

        You will feel sleepier than usual at first

        That means it’s working.

        Don’t drive when you feel drowsy. If sleepiness hits behind the wheel, pull over somewhere safe and rest.
